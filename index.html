What is Adsterra Auto Bot?

Adsterra Auto Bot is a desktop automation tool that opens multiple browser sessions and loads your Adsterra smart/direct links in the background. It is designed to help you generate repeated visits to your own links with minimal effort.

  • Runs locally on Windows as main.exe — no server or account needed.
  • Configurable — set the number of visits and concurrent browser windows (threads) based on your RAM.
  • Three modes — Headless (invisible), Show Browser, or Hide off-screen.
  • Multi-link support — add several smartlinks and let the bot cycle through them.
  • Free to use — the author includes 2 of their own links in the rotation so both parties earn.
